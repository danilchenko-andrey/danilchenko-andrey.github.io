Photo & video backup for iOS

Your originals.
Your Google Drive.

JustSaved backs up original photos, videos and Live Photo components from your iPhone to a folder in your own Google Drive.

Your originals stay in Photos. Uploads go directly from your device to Google Drive, without a JustSaved server in between.

Read the Privacy PolicyContact support

A simple backup routine

  1. Connect your Google account.
  2. Name your backup folder and allow access to Photos.
  3. Choose your accessible library or only items added after setup, and choose Wi-Fi-only or mobile uploads.

Stay in control

  • Save original files without recompression.
  • Pause, resume and retry uploads.
  • See your queue and completed-item counts.
  • Sign out without deleting your Google Drive backups.

Good to know

Requires iOS 18.5 or later and a Google account with available Drive storage. iOS controls background activity, so open JustSaved regularly to discover new items and keep backups moving. Backup is not continuous or guaranteed while the app is closed.

Drive stores ordinary original files, not a restorable Photos library. Live Photos are saved as separate photo and video components; albums, edits and other Photos-library organization are not recreated.

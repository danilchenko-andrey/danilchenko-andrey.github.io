JustSaved for iOS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

JustSaved is developed by Andrei Danilchenko. It backs up original media from your Photos library to your Google Drive. This policy explains the information used by the app and this website.

Photos and videos

With your permission, JustSaved reads the Photos items available to it, including original photo and video files, filenames, media types, creation dates and local asset identifiers. Limited Photos access limits the items the app can discover.

The app exports originals without recompression and uploads them to Google Drive. Original files may contain embedded information such as capture dates, camera details or location; JustSaved does not strip that metadata. Live Photos include separate still-image and video components. If an original is stored in iCloud, Apple's Photos service may download it to your device before backup. JustSaved does not delete originals from Photos.

Google account and Drive access

JustSaved uses Google Sign-In. Google provides sign-in information, which can include your account identifier, email address and basic profile. The app uses your account identifier to keep backup state separate for each account and displays your email address in Settings. Authentication tokens are managed by the Google Sign-In SDK using the iOS Keychain, not stored in the app's backup-queue file. JustSaved does not receive your Google password.

In addition to Google Sign-In's basic identity access, JustSaved requests the Google Drive permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This allows access to files created by or explicitly opened with the app, rather than unrestricted access to your Drive. JustSaved creates or reuses its backup folder, uploads files, and reads app-accessible file metadata to find and verify backups.

Google Drive receives the original files, filenames, file types and sizes, together with app-specific metadata: a content checksum, a hashed local asset identifier and a JustSaved marker. These support upload verification and duplicate detection. Uploads go directly from your device to Google over HTTPS. There is no JustSaved-operated backend that receives or stores your media or Google account data.

Information stored on your device

JustSaved keeps account-specific settings, folder identifiers, local asset identifiers, filenames, checksums, upload status, completion history and error information on your device. It also temporarily stages original files and, when needed, remaining upload bytes. Resumable upload addresses are retained so interrupted uploads can continue.

This app data uses iOS file protection and is excluded from device backups. Staged files are removed after successful verification or during sign-out; interrupted work can leave temporary files until later cleanup. Queue and history data remain on the device after sign-out so the same account can continue later.

Your choices and deletion

  • You can change Photos access in iOS Settings, pause backup, choose Wi-Fi-only uploads or sign out in JustSaved.
  • Signing out stops new work, cancels background upload requests and clears the app's Google sign-in session. A request already accepted by Google may still finish remotely. Signing out is not the same as revoking the app's Google authorization.
  • You can revoke Google access through your Google Account connections.
  • To remove local queue and history data, delete the app from your device rather than offloading it. Sign out first to clear its sign-in session.
  • Backups remain in Google Drive until you delete them there. Signing out, revoking access or deleting JustSaved does not delete those files. Manage deletion and Trash in Google Drive.

iOS controls background execution. Wi-Fi-only applies to Drive uploads; an iCloud download already in progress may continue after a network change. For a strict no-cellular policy for Photos downloads, also disable Photos cellular data in iOS Settings.

Third-party services and diagnostics

The app does not include advertising, behavioral analytics or a third-party crash-reporting service. Google Sign-In and Google Drive are third-party services. Google states that its Sign-In SDK may collect a user identifier to record authorized access and an IP address for fraud prevention. Google's handling of information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy; see also Google Sign-In's data disclosure. Apple's Photos, iCloud and operating-system services are subject to Apple's privacy policies.

JustSaved uses Google user data only to provide its backup features. It does not use that data for advertising, sell it or use it to train general-purpose AI models. JustSaved's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

This website and support

These JustSaved pages do not add analytics scripts, advertising or cookies. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to send and uses it to respond. Please do not send passwords, authentication tokens or private photos.
